hisia.live is a radio that plays around the clock and follows the sunset around the world. Every track is generated by AI from one city's weekly chart: its tempo, key, instruments and genre, turned into a text prompt. No recording goes in, every artist and title is invented, and the music is free to use under CC BY 4.0.

These are its 650 tracks ranked by a music-preference model, at most two per city, so the list covers 53 cities.

Ranked 4 Nov 2026 by TuneJury A1. Press play on any rank; the bar carries on down the chart from there.

How this was ranked, and how far to trust it

Each track was scored by TuneJury A1 (paper), a model trained to predict which of two clips people prefer. The score is the average over three 10-second windows of the track.

This is the model's opinion, not a measure of quality. It agreed with our own blind listening 8 times in 12, too few to call. It does not predict chart success: a track's score is unrelated to how long the song it mirrors stays on its chart. It prefers some cities' sound: city alone explains a third of the scores.

So the list is capped at two tracks per city. Without the cap the top 100 covers only 38 cities, Hong Kong 7, Lima 6. The cap drops 31 of those tracks and brings in 15 more cities. Six tracks hidden on the radio for audible stutters are left out.
